## Brand & Style
The design system establishes an empowering, forward-looking, and high-trust learning ecosystem for ambitious Indonesian youth and early-career professionals. Blending modern corporate clarity with dynamic educational energy, the aesthetic draws upon contemporary neo-digital product paradigms: hyper-legible typography, generous whitespace, crisp structural delineation, and warm gamified accents.

Visual expressions balance approachable mentorship and pragmatic career outcomes. The tone is encouraging, competent, and structured—avoiding academic stiffness while delivering clarity and rigor across intense coursework, live bootcamps, and peer exchange.

## Colors
The palette pairs a foundational electric indigo primary with a focused teal cyan secondary and an energizing warm amber tertiary.

- **Primary (`#1F46F5`)**: Drives main user journeys, primary call-to-actions, active navigation highlights, and progress completions.
- **Secondary (`#0E9AA7`)**: Derived from digital mentorship cues; serves as the signature accent for verified certifications, technical pathways, and supportive interface markers.
- **Tertiary (`#F97316`)**: Reserved for motivational elements, streaks, hot cohort markers, and time-sensitive achievements.
- **Neutral (`#0F172A`)**: Rich slate anchoring typography and iconography, ensuring optimal contrast and readability.
- **Surface Foundations**: Light backgrounds utilize clean slate whites (`#F8FAFC` and `#FFFFFF`) framed by precise geometric borders (`#E2E8F0`) to maintain clear modular cards.

## Typography
The system adopts a high-efficiency dual-type pairing:

- **Headings & Key Metrics**: **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides open counters, contemporary geometric rhythms, and high legibility across mobile title headers, milestone banners, and track titles.
- **Body & Component Labels**: **Inter** delivers crisp, non-distracting rendering across dense cohort syllabi, assignment briefs, and threaded community discussions.

## Layout & Spacing
Built primarily around a mobile-first fluid layout extending gracefully to multi-column desktop dashboards.

- **Mobile Viewports (under 768px)**: Strict 4-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) outer margins and 16px gutters, maximizing thumb reach and card scanability.
- **Tablet/Desktop Viewports**: Transitions to an 8-column (tablet) and 12-column (desktop) layout with 24px (`1.5rem`) margins and 24px gutters, capping core content width at 1200px.
- **Vertical Rhythm**: Built using an incremental 4px/8px scale for predictable element spacing, tightly grouping related metadata while leaving breathing room between discrete modules.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth relies on crisp borders and soft tonal layering rather than heavy drop shadows:

- **Surface Layering**: The canvas sits on neutral slate (`#F8FAFC`). Primary cards and interactive panels sit on pure `#FFFFFF` bounded by an exact 1px outline of `#E2E8F0`.
- **Card Depth**: Standard cards utilize a subtle, ambient drop shadow (`0px 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`) with an anchor border to preserve crisp visual definition on high-DPI mobile screens.
- **Hover & Active Elevation**: Interactive states elevate smoothly via subtle vertical offset and a soft secondary shadow (`0px 6px 16px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`).
- **Modals & Overlays**: Floating sheets and bottom drawers utilize a 32% black backdrop tint combined with a soft elevated shadow (`0px 12px 32px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`).

## Shapes
The shape philosophy balances crisp structural containers with pill-shaped active elements:

- **Cards & Inputs**: Feature standard rounded corners (`0.5rem` / 8px) to establish organized modular blocks.
- **Badges, Tags, and Filters**: Full pill radius (`9999px`) to emphasize tapability and thematic categories (e.g., skill tags, cohort status, verified mentor indicators).
- **Modal Sheets**: Bottom sheets incorporate top corner radii (`1rem` / 16px) for an organic handheld touch feel.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid electric indigo background (`#1F46F5`) with crisp white label (`#FFFFFF`). Min height 48px on mobile for thumb accessibility; 8px rounded corners.
- **Secondary**: Clear border (`1.5px solid #1F46F5`) with `#1F46F5` text and transparent background; fills with a 5% indigo tint on press.
- **Tertiary & Gamified**: Orange fill (`#F97316`) for milestone claims, bootcamps registration alerts, or scholarship application buttons.

### Chips & Skill Track Badges
- **Skill Pill**: Compact 24px–28px height, rounded-full styling. Uses light tint surfaces (`#E4FEFF` or `#FEF3C7`) with contrasting high-saturation text (`#0E9AA7` or `#B45309`).
- **Mentor Verification Tag**: Pill chip pairing a micro emerald checkmark icon, bold text, and a soft emerald surface (`rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.1)`).

### Cards
- **Course / Bootcamp Cards**: Pure white surface, 1px border (`#E2E8F0`), 8px rounded corners, 16px padding. Features media thumbnail, skill track pill, milestone progress bar, mentor badge, and pricing/enrollment CTA.
- **Community Discussion Cards**: Thread-style card featuring author metadata, mentor badge, timestamp, post snippet, upvote pill counter, and comment reply tally.

### Inputs & Form Fields
- Single-line fields with 48px height, 1px neutral border (`#CBD5E1`), 8px radius, and `#0F172A` input text. Focus state switches border to `#1F46F5` with a subtle 2px outer indigo ring (`rgba(31, 70, 245, 0.15)`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- 20px controls with a 4px corner radius for checkboxes, full circle for radios. Active state is solid `#1F46F5` with high-contrast white glyph.

### Progress & Milestone Meters
- 6px height tracks with rounded ends. Base is neutral slate (`#E2E8F0`); fill is primary indigo or amber accent for XP/streak rewards.
